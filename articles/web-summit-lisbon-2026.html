ELISENCE at Web Summit Lisbon 2026: What Happens Between Appointments?

By Sam Asi, Founder of ELISENCE · 9 November 2026

This photo was taken when we arrived in Lisbon. It marks the start of our time at Web Summit and a chance to put a question we’ve been working on at ELISENCE to people who see it from different sides: what happens to a person’s health information between appointments?

Imagine leaving hospital with a discharge summary and a change to your medicines. At home, you might keep notes about how you feel, check a reminder on your phone, and wait to hear who will arrange your follow-up. Each piece of information has a purpose. The hard part is making sense of them together when you need to decide what to do next or who to contact.

That everyday gap is one reason we’re building ELISENCE. We want to explore how people and families can keep track of the information that matters to them and make better use of it in conversations with their care teams. In Lisbon, I’d like to hear from people building these tools and from those who would have to use them. Their answers may be very different.

My question for this week is simple: Between one appointment and the next, what’s the one piece of information you wish you didn’t have to chase?
